MasterSelects is a browser-based video editor and compositor built on
WebGPU. Everything — timeline editing, effects, motion design,
replicators, audio, and export — runs locally in your browser on your
GPU. Projects and media stay on your machine.

The editor lives at
[www.masterselects.com](https://www.masterselects.com) and runs in any
browser with WebGPU support (Chrome and Edge today). There is nothing to
install; an optional native helper adds the few capabilities browsers
still lack.
